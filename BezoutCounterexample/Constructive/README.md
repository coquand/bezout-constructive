# Constructive pieces of the direct refutation (see ~/COLL/bezout-direct.tex)

Mathlib-based, but every proof here is checked to use only constructive steps:

* `scripts/ConstructiveAudit.lean` (`lake env lean scripts/ConstructiveAudit.lean`) traverses all
  declarations of `BezoutCounterexample.Constructive.*`, stops at Mathlib constants and at
  `@[cprim]` primitives, fails on `Classical.*`, `sorryAx`, the classical decidability instances of
  `ℝ`, and on any external constant not in `docs/constructive-whitelist.txt`.
  Report: `docs/constructive-audit.md`.
* Whitelist discipline: an external constant is added only after checking that its *statement* is
  constructively valid at the types used (its Mathlib proof may be classical). New constants
  appear as `NOT WHITELISTED` failures and must be reviewed before being appended.
* `@[cprim]` (in `Attr.lean`): our own lemmas with constructively valid statements and classical
  proofs; listed with their statements in the report.

Files:

* `Moebius.lean` — `discrete_moebius` (Lemma 2.2 of the note): a closed chain with reversed
  half-angle and the relation `μ (p,q) M (u,v)ᵀ = 1` is contradictory. Finite sign search; no
  division on `ℝ`, no proof of a strict inequality by contradiction (`linarith` on `<` goals
  compiles to `lt_of_not_ge`, i.e. Markov's principle, and is avoided).
  Primitives: `sign_split` (sign decided with a margin), `le_of_not_lt'` (`≤` is `¬ >`).
* `DualityP.lean` — `PChart.mem_F_iff` (Proposition 3.2): in any `ℚ`-algebra with
  `∂ᵢ xₖ = δᵢₖ` and weights `≥ 1`, `g ∈ F_t ↔ ∂^α g ∈ 𝔭` for all `α` with `w·α < t`.
  No Krull, no Noetherian or local hypothesis, derivations need not commute.
* `ChartP.lean` — `mem_F_iff_P`: the same duality for HM's charts `Chart R n` and their ideals
  `Chart.F w t`, with zero weights allowed (head ideal `(xᵢ : wᵢ ≠ 0)`, head-supported `β`). The
  head is enumerated explicitly (`exists_enum`, a filter of `List.finRange n`) and becomes a
  `PChart` (`subP`); HM's commuting `Chart.D` is matched with list derivatives (`Dl_subP`).
* `KrullP.lean` — Krull intersection (Perdry, Monthly 2004; Kuroki's Rees-ring form, Prop. 6.2 of
  `~/COLL/kunz-constructive.pdf`): `mem_mul_of_rees` (one stationarity relation in `R[It]` gives
  `b ∈ bI`), `eq_zero_of_iInf` (`⋂ Iᵏ = 0` for `I ⊆ Jac`). The Noetherian input is the single
  named hypothesis `SeqCond (reesSubring I)`: every sequence meets the ideal of its predecessors
  (Richman–Seidenberg / Coquand–Persson; Hilbert basis theorem in Perdry–Schuster, MSCS 2013).
  Intended to replace `Ideal.iInf_pow_eq_bot_of_isLocalRing` in (F1) and (F2).

* `NoethBar.lean` — inductive Noetherianity `IndNoeth A := NBar []` (bar form: Richman,
  Seidenberg, Jacobsson–Löfwall, Coquand–Persson). `IndNoeth.seqCond` (bar ⇒ `SeqCond`);
  transfer along surjections (`IndNoeth.of_surjective`) and localisations
  (`IndNoeth.of_isLocalization`) with no choice: each bar step lifts one element. The sequence
  form `SeqCond` would need countable choice for both transfers, which is why the bar form is the
  primary notion. `indNoeth_of_field` (decidable equality). `reesHom_surjective`: the Rees ring of
  `(x₁,…,x_k)` is an image of `A[T₁,…,T_k]`. `seqCond_rees_of_finiteType`: `SeqCond` for the Rees
  ring of any f.g. ideal of `R ⧸ J`, `R` a localisation of a f.g. `ℚ`-algebra, from the single
  hypothesis `PolyBar := ∀ N, IndNoeth ℚ[Y₁,…,Y_N]`.
* `AlmostFull.lean` — almost-full relations (Vytiniotis–Coquand–Wahlstedt): `AF.mono`, `AF.comap`,
  intersection `AF.inter` via `oplus₀/₁/₂` (intuitionistic Ramsey, no choice), `af_nat_le`
  (decidable `≤` on ℕ), `af_finsupp_le` (Dickson's lemma on `Fin N →₀ ℕ`). Fully constructive.
* `TopReduce.lean` — `top_reduce`: top reduction of `f ∈ ℚ[Y₁,…,Y_N]` by a finite list `G` of
  nonzero polynomials (lex order): some `r ≡ f mod (G)` is `0` or has leading monomial divisible
  by no leading monomial of `G`. Well-founded induction on the lex degree; decisions are `f = 0`
  and a finite search (decidable equality of `ℚ`, decidable `≤` on `ℕ`).
* `SatBar.lean` — `IndNoeth.eq_zero_of_divisible_torsion`: in an inductively Noetherian ring,
  if every `y` with `P y` is `s`-divisible within `P` and `s`-torsion, then `y = 0`. Perdry's
  argument along the bar (invariant `y₀ = sⁿ h`, `sⁿ · older ⊆ (s y₀)`), no choice. Replaces
  the Nakayama step (f.g. of `K̄`) of `ReesSmooth.reesPsi_ker`, which with `rees_formallySmooth`
  now takes `[Fact (PolyIndNoeth S)]` instead of `[IsNoetherianRing S]`.
* `FGKer.lean` — `ker_eq_span_tableRel`, `fg_ker_of_basis`: for `φ : ℚ[Y₁,…,Y_N] → L` with
  `L` having a finite basis `B` lifted by polynomials `bₖ`, `ker φ` is generated by the explicit
  multiplication-table relations (canonical representatives via `B.repr`, induction on `p`; no
  Hilbert basis theorem). `fg_ker_of_quotient_basis` (points of f.t. algebras), `fg_map_atPrime`.
  Supplies the `FG` arguments of `exists_centred` at explicit points (not yet wired in).
  `Localization.AtPrime.isLocalRing` and `Ideal.IsMaximal.isPrime'` are deliberately not
  whitelisted (their statements are disjunctions needing decidable membership).
* `PolyBar.lean` — `polyBar : PolyBar` (Coquand–Persson), **fully constructive** (2026-10-02):
  induction on `af_finsupp_le`, carrying besides the list `l` the list `G ⊆ (l)` of nonzero
  remainders produced so far, with invariant `T x y → x ≤ y ∨ DvG G x`. Each step top-reduces the
  new element by `G`; a zero remainder gives `a ∈ (l)`. `G` need not be a Gröbner basis of `(l)`,
  so neither Buchberger nor a normal form modulo `(l)` is needed; the former `@[cprim]`
  `normal_form` (which had Gröbner strength) is deleted.

* `KrullBar.lean` — Krull intersection for an **arbitrary** ideal (Lemma 6.3 of
  `~/COLL/kunz-constructive.pdf`, 2026-10-03): `PolyIndNoeth.mem_mul_of_mem_iInf_pow`
  (`d ∈ ⋂ Iⁿ → ∃ c ∈ I, d = c d`, no generators of `I`), `eq_zero_of_mem_iInf_pow` (`1 - c` a
  unit on `I`), `eq_bot_of_le_sq`, and `le_of_le_sup_sq` (`I ≤ K ⊔ I²` ⇒ `I ≤ K`: Nakayama without
  finite generation, via `B ⧸ K`). "Do as if": outer bar of `B` on lists `σ ⊆ I` with invariant
  `Good σ ∨ d ∈ I d`; in the bar step, inner bar of the Rees ring of `⟨σ⟩` (`indNoeth_rees_lspan`)
  on `d, dt, …, dtᵏ⁻¹`; extending needs `d ∈ ⟨σ⟩ᵏ`, obtained from `d ∈ Iᵏ` by `pow_mem_or`
  (`{x | x ∈ Jᵏ ∨ D}` is an ideal, so finitely many consultations of the escape branch, no
  lists of generators). A good list gives `d ∈ J d` by a coefficient invariant
  (`coeff_mem_of_mem_lspan`: the `q`-th coefficient lies in `J^(q+1-m) · (d)`). No choice.
